# [Submitted to ICASSP 2027] Neural Surrogate Modeling of Ultrasonic Wave Propagation in Heterogeneous Media

We study neural surrogate modeling for ultrasonic wave propagation in heterogeneous media, comparing U-Net, FNO, and U-NO across training and higher spatial resolutions. We propose **LU-NO**, a U-shaped neural operator that combines Fourier layers with learned local-integral resampling. We observed improved cross-resolution generalization, particularly for reflected and scattered waves.
